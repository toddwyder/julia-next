# Shipping judges blind; a broken review costs nothing

Shipping receives only the card and its pull request, runs the change itself, and posts PASS, PASS+NOTES or FAIL; PASS+NOTES advances like PASS. A review that ends without a well-formed verdict is unreviewed: the FAIL count holds and the card goes to Todd. Why: the judge must never take the builder's word, and a review that broke says nothing about the work. A check Shipping could not run also makes the review unreviewed, not a FAIL.
